How does the part come free?

Station 1: contour, hole and slot go below Z10.

Station 2: the back is faced down to Z8.974.

Removing the bottom opens those cuts right through and the part is free.

G41 D1: mind the register!

The path is already on the cutter centre.

In D1 / D2 / D6: wear only ≈ 0

With the radius in: profile +6.35 mm, hole and slot −6.35 mm. Haas: Setting 40.

Other warnings

  • Back: Fusion starts at Z16.3, but is the part 19.05 tall? → 4.5 mm first pass
  • T7 without M8
  • One zero for two vises
  • “Rapids” G1 F5000: 40 s instead of 8

The key-seat cutter

It cuts sideways, under an edge: it makes the tooth that levers the cap.

It drops into the open slot, then moves in sideways with G41 D6.

Lots of edge engaged → 1,500 rpm and 152–305 mm/min.

Positive Z?

Top face at Z19.05, finished back at Z8.974.

Z zero is below the part (probably on the parallels plane): that way it works for both vises.
